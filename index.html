About

I started in QA engineering, which means I spent years finding the exact moment a product breaks. That habit stuck. I design with edge cases, empty states, and real data in mind from the start, not as cleanup at the end.

These days I work across B2B and B2C: ERP and POS systems on one side, consumer mobile apps on the other. I care a lot about design systems and the tooling around them, and I've been building AI-assisted workflows that connect design decisions straight to Figma tokens.

Most of my work ships in Arabic and English, so RTL isn't an afterthought for me. It's the default.
